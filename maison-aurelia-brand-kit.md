# Maison Aurelia — Brand Kit

**Status:** Draft concept for the future rebrand (new property, self-hosted, per Matt's message). Not yet applied to the live site. This is the reference document to work from when that phase starts.

---

## 1. The name

**Maison Aurelia**

- *Maison* — French for "house." Instant couture/atelier signal (Maison Margiela, Maison Francis Kurkdjian) — positions this as a house, not a clinic chain.
- *Aurelia* — Latin root *aureus*, "golden." Also a real historical name (Aurelia Cotta, mother of Julius Caesar) — timeless, elegant, carries the gold theme without ever having to say the word "gold."
- Together: **"The Golden House."** Captures the elixir-of-eternal-youth mood Paul wanted, entirely through etymology — no borrowed character, no IP exposure, no legal risk.

**Why this clears the bar the Lisle Von Rhoman idea didn't:** nothing here is owned by anyone. It's a coined name built from public-domain Latin and French, the same way most premium fragrance/fashion houses actually name themselves.

---

## 2. Brand story (for About/hero copy, later)

> Gold doesn't fade. It doesn't rust, dull, or perish — it's simply protected, polished, preserved. Maison Aurelia is built on that idea: skin doesn't need to be chased into something artificial. It needs to be protected, preserved, and given back its own shine.

Keep this as **mood, not medical claim**. The moment "elixir" or "eternal" language starts implying an actual clinical outcome (rather than a brand feeling), it crosses into ASA territory — same rule that already governs the current site's copy. The aesthetic can be as theatrical as we like; the treatment copy underneath stays as factual and consultation-led as it is now.

## 3. Colour palette

Art deco jewel tones — deep, saturated, metallic. A real departure from the current petrol-teal system, and from the "pale gold" template cliché both.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Emerald** (primary dark) | `#0B3D2E` | Backgrounds, header/footer, deep sections |
| **Emerald deep** | `#082A20` | Darkest shade — near-black green, hero backgrounds |
| **Aurelia gold** | `#C9A227` | Primary accent — headlines, dividers, icon linework |
| **Gold bright** | `#D4AF37` | Hover states, foil-effect highlights |
| **Onyx** | `#0A0A0A` | Deco linework, high-contrast text on ivory |
| **Ivory / parchment** | `#F8F4E9` | Light backgrounds — warmer and more "antique paper" than the current porcelain cream |
| **Ivory deep** | `#EDE6D3` | Card backgrounds, subtle section breaks |

Rule of thumb: **emerald + ivory** carry most of the page; **gold** is used the way foil is used on a real deco invitation — thin lines, small caps labels, icon strokes, never as a large fill.

## 4. Typography

All available on Google Fonts (keeps it deployable under the same constraints as the current build).

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Logotype / wordmark** | [Poiret One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poiret+One) | *The* geometric deco revival font — ultra-thin, iconic 1920s. Use only for the "MAISON AURELIA" logotype itself, letter-spaced wide. Too thin for anything else. |
| **Headings** | [Cinzel](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel) | Roman-inspired serif, engraved/monumental feel — deco-adjacent without being a novelty font. Carries real weight for H1/H2s, unlike Poiret. |
| **Body / UI** | [Jost](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jost) | Futura-inspired geometric sans — genuinely deco-period-accurate (Futura was designed in 1927), and fully legible at body-copy sizes, unlike most "deco" fonts. |

This replaces Bodoni Moda + Manrope (the current system) entirely — Bodoni is high-fashion-editorial, not deco; it wouldn't sit right against gold linework and geometric motifs.

## 5. Motifs & ornamentation

Deco identity leans on *pattern and line*, not photography tricks:

- **Sunburst / fan** shapes — radiating gold lines, used sparingly behind hero content or as a section divider
- **Stepped / ziggurat** geometry — echoed in card corners, button shapes, or a repeating border motif
- **Thin gold hairlines** — single-pixel gold rules to separate sections, instead of today's soft grey borders
- **Monogram roundel** — an "MA" (Maison Aurelia) monogram in a circular geometric frame, doing the job the current badge/wings crest does — this would be the new icon/favicon
- **Small caps + wide letter-spacing** — for labels/kickers (`PROTECT & PRESERVE`-style eyebrow text), very on-genre for deco signage

Avoid: gradients (deco is flat and metallic, not soft), rounded/organic shapes, anything that reads as "spa" rather than "house."

---

## 6. Tone of voice

- Confident, a little theatrical, never breathless — think a 1930s department store announcement, not a TikTok ad
- Short, declarative sentences carry the drama; the supporting copy stays plain and clinical (this is where "protect & preserve" — the existing tagline — actually still fits perfectly under a deco identity)
- No literal immortality/miracle language — the *name* carries the myth, the *copy* stays honest

## 7. What carries over from Face Crease Police

The clinical substance doesn't change with the rebrand — only the aesthetic wrapper:

- Jo's real credentials, NMC registration, independent prescriber status
- Consultation-first, no-hard-sell positioning
- All ASA/CAP-safe copy rules for prescription-only medicines (unchanged, non-negotiable regardless of brand)
- "Protect & Preserve" — genuinely survives the rebrand as a tagline; it already reads as deco-appropriate
